## British Airways American Express Premium Plus: earn rate cut to 1.25 Avios per £1 (UK)

- **Country:** UK
- **Category:** Credit cards / loyalty programs
- **What changed:** Amex UK's product page now lists the Premium Plus card at 1.25 Avios per £1 on purchases, with no separate higher rate for British Airways spend. The annual fee is still £300 and the Companion Voucher still needs £15,000 of spend per cardmembership year.
  - Head for Points reports the cut took effect on Oct 7, 2026, from 1.5 Avios per £1, and that the 3 Avios per £1 on BA and BA Holidays spend was dropped at the same time.
  - Also reported (lead): from 1 April 2027, Premium Plus tier points are earned as you spend (1 per £10, annual cap raised from 2,500 to 3,000) instead of at the £15k/£20k/£25k thresholds.
- **What it means for a viewer:** A UK Premium Plus holder now earns about 17% fewer Avios on everyday spending (1.25 instead of 1.5 per £1), so on £15,000 a year that's 18,750 Avios instead of 22,500. The Companion Voucher becomes an even bigger share of the card's value, and the card makes less sense for anyone who won't hit £15,000.
- **Status:** verified (current earn rate 1.25 per £1, £300 fee, £15,000 voucher spend, on the Amex UK page). Lead: the Oct 7 effective date, the old 1.5 and 3x rates, and the April 2027 tier-point changes.
- **Sources:** https://www.americanexpress.com/uk/credit-cards/ba-premium-plus-credit-card/ · lead: https://www.headforpoints.com/2026/10/05/british-airways-american-express-premium-plus-changes/
- **Short angle:** UK only, so weak on its own. Possible fit for the idea bank's "premium annual-fee cards: the break-even math": £300 fee against 1.25 Avios per £1 plus the voucher, with "in the UK" on screen. Avios valuations must be researched on production day.
