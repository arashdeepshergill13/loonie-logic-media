## British Airways Executive Club: higher tier thresholds from April 1, 2027

- **Country:** UK and worldwide (BA Executive Club members anywhere)
- **Category:** Loyalty programs (elite status)
- **What changed:** BA's tier-points page now lists thresholds "from 1 April 2027": Bronze 3,500 (unchanged), Silver 8,000 (from 7,500), Gold 21,000 (from 20,000). It also lists milestone rewards from April 1, 2027: 2,500 bonus Avios at 5,750 tier points, 4,000 at 12,000 and 5,000 at 17,000. The current collection year (April 2026 to March 2027) stays at Silver 7,500 and Gold 20,000.
- **What it means for a viewer:** BA status gets about 7% harder for Silver and 5% harder for Gold from the 2027-28 year. Avios earning and award prices aren't part of this change.
- **Status:** verified (thresholds and milestones). Not verified: blog reports (Oct 28) of smaller bonus tier points on Club World and flexible First, with old rates kept for bookings made by Nov 25, 2026 for travel before April 1, 2027; no Nov 25 deadline appears on BA's page.
- **Sources:** https://www.britishairways.com/content/executive-club/about-the-club/tiers-and-benefits/about-tier-points · lead: https://www.airtraveler.club/news/british-airways-raises-executive-club-silver-8000-points/
- **Short angle:** "BA Silver costs 500 more tier points next year": worked math on what the extra 500 or 1,000 tier points mean in flights. Re-check BA's flight tier-point table before giving any per-flight figure (not checked tonight). Don't use the Nov 25 deadline unless BA confirms it.

## Aeroplan Black Friday card offer (Canada): 50% more points on spend, capped at 20,000

- **Country:** Canada
- **Category:** Credit cards (welcome offer)
- **What changed:** Air Canada's American Express Aeroplan cards page shows a "Black Friday 2026 Card Acquisition Offer": 50% more points on eligible purchases, up to 20,000 bonus points. Applications from 12:00 AM ET Nov 12 to 11:59 PM PT Nov 30, 2026; eligible spend from Nov 12, 2026 to Jan 15, 2027; only for people who have never held a primary Aeroplan credit card and who apply through Air Canada's channels (not Amex's). Bonus points post within 12 weeks after the spend period. Standing welcome offers on the same page: Reserve up to 85,000 (60,000 after $7,500 in 3 months plus 25,000 for $2,500 in month 13), Business Reserve up to 90,000, Core up to 45,000; "subject to change at any time", no end date shown.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Only first-time Aeroplan cardholders in Canada qualify, and the extra points depend on spending by Jan 15. It stacks on top of the regular welcome offer, per InsideFlyer (not confirmed on the page).
- **Status:** verified (Air Canada page; the lower part of the page was not read in full)
- **Sources:** https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/aeroplan/credit-cards/amex/personal.html · lead: https://www.insideflyer.com/posts/aeroplan-launches-37-black-friday-offers/
