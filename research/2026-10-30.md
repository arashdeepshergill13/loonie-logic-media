## Bank of Canada raises its policy rate to 2.5%

- **Country:** Canada
- **Category:** Interest rates
- **What changed:** On Oct 28, 2026 the Bank of Canada raised the overnight rate target 25 basis points, from 2.25% to 2.5%. The Bank Rate is now 2.75% and the deposit rate 2.45%. It's the first hike after the cuts of Sep 17 and Oct 29, 2025. The Bank pointed to high oil prices and CPI running near 3%, which it expects to reach about 3.5% by year-end, and said more increases could be needed. Next announcement: Dec 9, 2026.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Variable-rate mortgages and lines of credit in Canada get more expensive right away. Savings and GIC rates may tick up, but banks often pass hikes on to savers slowly, so it's worth checking whether your savings rate actually moves.
- **Status:** verified
- **Sources:** https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2026/10/fad-press-release-2026-10-28/ · https://www.bankofcanada.ca/core-functions/monetary-policy/key-interest-rate/
- **Short angle:** Part of a multi-country "rates are going up again" Short (see the global rates item below).

## Aeroplan points start expiring again on Nov 30, 2026

- **Country:** Canada-based program, with members worldwide (including US members)
- **Category:** Loyalty programs
- **What changed:** Air Canada paused Aeroplan points expiry until Nov 30, 2026. From that date the normal policy returns: points expire after 18 months with no activity. Activity means earning, redeeming, donating, transferring or converting at least one point. Aeroplan says it emails up to 60 days before points expire. Points don't expire while you're a primary TD, American Express or CIBC Aeroplan cardholder or hold Aeroplan Elite Status. Expired points can be reinstated within 6 months by buying them back, flying Air Canada or getting an Aeroplan credit card.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Anyone with an Aeroplan balance and no activity since mid-2025 should earn or move a single point before Nov 30. One small transaction resets the 18-month clock.
- **Status:** verified
- **Sources:** https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/aeroplan/news/points-expiry-suspended.html · https://www.aircanada.com/us/en/aco/home/aeroplan/your-aeroplan/inactivity-policy.html
- **Short angle:** "Your Aeroplan points start dying on November 30." The 18-month rule, the one-point fix, and who's exempt. It fills the idea bank's "points expiry" slot with a real date.

## Amex US: each new card now gets its own Membership Rewards account

- **Country:** US
- **Category:** Loyalty programs / credit cards
- **What changed:** An amendment in the US Membership Rewards terms, effective immediately (reported Oct 15, 2026), reads: "When you open a new Product, a new Rewards Account will be created and linked to that Product." Points from a newly opened card no longer land automatically in your existing balance. The same terms say that cancelling the last card linked to a rewards account forfeits that account's points immediately. Existing cards keep their current accounts. A secondary source says the accounts can still be combined online, in the app or by phone, but that wasn't confirmed on the terms page.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Before closing or downgrading a newer Amex card in the US, check which rewards account its points sit in and move them first, or you can lose them even though you still hold another Membership Rewards card.
- **Status:** verified (the new-account clause and the forfeiture rule). The combine-by-phone detail is a lead.
- **Sources:** https://www.americanexpress.com/us/rewards/membership-rewards/terms/ · lead: https://viewfromthewing.com/?p=258466
- **Short angle:** "Closing this Amex card can delete your points, even if you keep another one." A US-only Short; say "in the US" on screen.

## UK cash ISA limit falls to £12,000 from 6 April 2027

- **Country:** UK
- **Category:** Rules with dates
- **What changed:** From 6 April 2027 the annual cash ISA limit falls from £20,000 to £12,000 for people under 65. Savers aged 65 or over keep £20,000, and the overall ISA allowance stays at £20,000. Transfers from stocks and shares ISAs into cash ISAs will be restricted, and a 22% charge will apply to interest on cash held inside stocks and shares ISAs.
- **What it means for a viewer:** A UK saver under 65 who wants more than £12,000 a year tax-free in cash has until 5 April 2027 to use the full £20,000 in a cash ISA. After that, the extra £8,000 has to go into investments to stay tax-free.
- **Status:** verified
- **Sources:** https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/reduction-in-the-cash-individual-savings-account-isa-limit/cash-individual-savings-account-isa-limit-reduction
- **Short angle:** "UK savers lose £8,000 of tax-free cash room in April." The date, who's exempt, and the stocks-and-shares workaround and its 22% catch. UK only.
