## Chase Ultimate Rewards (US): Qantas and Cathay Pacific added as 1:1 transfer partners

- **Country:** US
- **Category:** Loyalty programs (new transfer partners)
- **What changed:** Reported Nov 18, 2026 (The Points Guy, NerdWallet, AwardWallet, Doctor of Credit, View from the Wing, Thrifty Traveler): Chase points now transfer 1:1, in 1,000-point steps, to Qantas Frequent Flyer and Cathay Pacific Asia Miles, effective Nov 18. AwardWallet lists eligible cards as Sapphire Preferred, Sapphire Reserve, Ink Business Preferred and Sapphire Reserve for Business (Freedom points via combining). First new Chase airline partners in years, per the reports. No launch bonus reported; transfer times not stated.
- **Not verified:** Chase's public transfer-partner education page fetched tonight still lists 10 airlines without Qantas or Cathay (the page looks out of date). The partner list in the Ultimate Rewards portal needs a login.
- **What it means for a viewer:** US Chase holders get two more oneworld programs, which matters most for booking oneworld partners (Cathay, Qantas, JAL, American) without going through Avios.
- **Status:** lead (multiple specialist outlets, no official page confirmed today)
- **Sources (leads):** https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-cathay-pacific-qantas-transfer-partners · https://awardwallet.com/news/chase-ultimate-rewards/chase-adds-qantas-cathay-transfer-partners · https://www.nerdwallet.com/travel/news/chase-transfer-partners-cathay-qantas · checked against https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/how-to-transfer-chase-ultimate-rewards-points (not listed)
- **Short angle (US, strong once verified):** "Chase points just got two new airlines": the case for a oneworld route that used to need Avios. Don't script until a Chase, Qantas or Cathay page confirms the partnership and ratio. Pair with `never-transfer-before-seat` (transfers are irreversible) rather than repeating it.

## UK CPI, October 2026: reported 3.6%

- **Country:** UK
- **Category:** Interest rates / inflation
- **What changed:** Moneyfacts (Nov 18) and MoneyWeek report CPI rose to 3.6% in the 12 months to October (3.5% in September), a fourth monthly rise; motor fuel up 33.2% and food inflation easing to 0.9%. ONS's release pages fetched tonight still said "not yet published" (likely cached), so the figure isn't confirmed.
- **What it means for a viewer:** If confirmed, UK prices are rising faster than September, which keeps a December Bank of England hike on the table (decision Dec 17) and leaves most easy-access savings below inflation.
- **Status:** lead
- **Sources (leads):** https://moneyfactscompare.co.uk/news/money/uk-inflation-october-2026/ · https://moneyweek.com/economy/news/live/inflation-cpi-october-2026-report · ONS (not yet showing): https://www.ons.gov.uk/releases/consumerpriceinflationukoctober2026
- **Short angle:** weak alone (savings-vs-inflation is made). Supporting figure once verified.
