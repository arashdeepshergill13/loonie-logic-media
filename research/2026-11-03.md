## IDFC FIRST Bank: zero forex markup on all credit cards (India)

- **Country:** India
- **Category:** Credit cards / money of travel
- **What changed:** IDFC FIRST Bank's own customer notice says zero forex markup applies to all IDFC FIRST credit cards from 9 September 2026. The bank says this saves up to 3.5% on international transactions. Earlier runs didn't record it.
  - **Reward points on international spending are unclear.** One official notice says international transactions stop earning reward points from 26 October 2026, except on listed cards (including IndiGo, Ashva and Mayura). A second official notice, dated 13 September, says the bank reversed that and points keep being earned "as applicable" to each card. A tracker (cardadvisor.in) lists cuts from Oct 26: no points on nine cards, IndiGo cut to 1 BluChip per ₹100, Ashva cut to 2X. Treat the points side as unresolved.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Indian IDFC FIRST cardholders no longer pay the usual markup of up to 3.5% on foreign spending, so for them a card abroad can now cost no more than at home in markup terms. Whether those foreign purchases still earn points depends on the card. Check before counting on them.
- **Status:** verified (zero forex markup from 9 Sep 2026, on IDFC FIRST's notice). Lead: the points on international spending after Oct 26 (the official notices conflict).
- **Sources:** https://www.idfcfirst.bank.in/content/dam/idfcfirstbank/pdf/credit-card/Credit-Card-Communication1-Sep-26.pdf · https://www.idfcfirst.bank.in/content/dam/idfcfirstbank/pdf/credit-card/Credit-Card-Communication-13-Sep-26.pdf · leads: https://www.insideflyer.com/posts/idfc-first-bank-cuts-forex-markup-to-zero/ · https://cardadvisor.in/changes/2026-W44
- **Short angle:** Supporting material, not a Short on its own. It fits the idea bank's "foreign transaction fees: the math on a two-week trip" as the India example: 3.5% markup on ₹1,00,000 of foreign spending is ₹3,500, against ₹0 on a zero-markup card. Pair it with a US or UK no-FX-fee example and name each country on screen.
