## Bilt: 2027 program changes, irreversible opt-out before your card anniversary

- **Country:** US
- **Category:** credit cards / loyalty
- **What changed (announced Oct 27, 2026):**
  - Bilt Cash now expires at the end of the month, 12 months after it's earned. The year-end expiry and rollover are gone.
  - On each cardholder's account anniversary, the Flexible Bilt Cash mode (4% back in Bilt Cash on everyday spend) is retired and everyone earns up to 1.25X points on rent or mortgage, unless they opt out in card settings before that anniversary. Opting out keeps the Flexible Bilt Cash mode and eligible annual credits, but it can't be switched afterwards.
  - From Jan 1, 2027, hotel redemptions with Bilt Cash go from monthly to twice a year: up to $50 each for Blue/Silver, $100 for Gold/Platinum, $200 for eligible Palladium.
  - Annual credits move to the card anniversary: Palladium's $200 Bilt Cash and $400 hotel credits combine into one $600 Bilt Cash credit; Obsidian's $100 hotel credit becomes $100 of Bilt Cash.
  - Daily Rewards start Jan 15, 2027 (one per on-time rent or mortgage payment); prize terms aren't published yet.
- **What it means:** US Bilt cardholders face a one-way choice with a personal deadline (their anniversary), and Bilt Cash balances now have a rolling 12-month clock. Check the anniversary date in the app.
- **Status:** verified (official newsroom page). Blog reports of a fixed Dec 31, 2026 deadline for new applicants are not on the official page; don't use them.
- **Sources:** https://newsroom.biltrewards.com/2027-bilt-program-updates
- **Short angle (US only):** "A one-way switch with no undo" — fits the irreversible-choice format; say "in the US" on screen and show the opt-out as the decision, not advice on which mode to pick.

## CRA 2027 registered plan limits (TFSA still not announced)

- **Country:** Canada
- **Category:** rules with dates
- **What changed:** CRA posted indexed limits on Oct 28, 2026. 2027 RRSP dollar limit $35,390 (2026: $33,810); 2028 RRSP limit $36,390. 2027 money purchase limit $36,390, DB limit $4,043.33, DPSP limit $18,195. 2027 YMPE $76,700, YAMPE $87,400. The 2027 TFSA dollar limit is not listed (last year's TFSA figure was posted Dec 1).
- **What it means:** Canadians can plan 2027 RRSP room now (still 18% of prior-year earned income up to the dollar limit). The TFSA forecasts ($7,500) remain unofficial.
- **Status:** verified
- **Sources:** https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/registered-plans-administrators/pspa/mp-rrsp-dpsp-tfsa-limits-ympe.html · https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/registered-plans-administrators/whats-new.html
