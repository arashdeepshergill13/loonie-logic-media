## Amex India: Membership Rewards points split by card from Nov 15, 2026

- **Country:** India
- **Category:** Loyalty programs / credit cards
- **What changed:** Amex India's support page now gives the date, so this item moves from lead to verified. From 15 November 2026, Membership Rewards points are kept separately for each Basic Card when a member holds two or more, and moving points between your own cards ends permanently. The same date removes the Membership Rewards Credit Card's 50% annual fee waiver, which was earned with spending of ₹90,000 or more in a card year. New supplementary card applications have been paused since 27 October 2026. Applications sent by 26 October still go through, but they must be completed by 3 November 2026.
- **What it means for a viewer:** An Indian Amex member with points spread across two cards should combine them onto the card they'll redeem from before Nov 15. After that date, points left on a card stay with that card.
- **Status:** verified
- **Sources:** https://www.americanexpress.com/en-in/support/digital/consumer-experience-updates/ · background (the current terms still describe linked accounts): https://www.americanexpress.com/en-in/rewards/membership-rewards/terms
- **Short angle:** "Amex is splitting your points by card." In the US, new cards now get their own rewards account and closing the last card on an account forfeits its points (see `2026-10-30.md`). In India, pooling ends Nov 15. Two countries, one fix: know which card your points sit on before you close or redeem anything. Name each country on screen.

## Revolut UK: the 5% boosted savings rate ends Dec 4, 2026

- **Country:** UK
- **Category:** Banks and fintechs / savings rates
- **What changed:** Revolut's 5% AER (variable) boosted rate went to new UK Instant Access Savings customers who signed up between 5 June and 4 August 2026. It applies to balances up to £25,000 and runs for six months, until 4 December 2026. After that, balances earn the plan's base rate: 2.9% AER on Standard, up to 4% on Ultra. Sign-ups for the offer have closed.
- **What it means for a viewer:** A Standard-plan saver holding £25,000 drops from about £1,250 a year in interest to about £725 a year on Dec 4, unless they move the money. These are simple-interest estimates at the advertised AERs. Any intro savings rate has a date when it ends, and that's the date to put in your calendar.
- **Status:** verified
- **Sources:** https://www.revolut.com/news/revolut_launches_market_leading_5_boosted_savings_rate_for_new_uk_customers/
- **Short angle:** "Your 5% savings rate has an end date." The intro-rate cliff, with Revolut UK as the real example (5% falling to 2.9% on Dec 4) and the £-per-year math. This fills the idea bank's "promo savings rates that drop" slot. Say "in the UK" on screen.
