## Wealthsimple savings account: terms confirmed (Canada)

- **Country:** Canada
- **Category:** Banks and fintechs
- **What changed:** Wealthsimple's own savings-account page (marked "Updated October 5, 2026") confirms the terms behind the 2.5% rate announced on Nov 5:
  - A flat 2.5% a year, calculated daily and paid monthly, on every dollar: no tiers, no minimum balance, no monthly or transfer fees.
  - Protection is through CIPF (eligible cash up to $1 million), not CDIC. The page itself compares this with the $100,000 CDIC limit at banks.
  - The savings account has no account or transit number, so pay can't be direct-deposited into it; you deposit to chequing and move it across.
- **What it means for a viewer:** For Canadians it's a simple, untiered 2.5%, which matches the Bank of Canada's 2.5% policy rate. If Canadian inflation is running above that (reported at 3.1% for September, see Leads), money in it is still losing purchasing power after inflation, and interest is taxable outside a TFSA or RRSP.
- **Status:** verified (rate, no tiers or fees, CIPF coverage, no direct deposit). Lead: Canada's September CPI.
- **Sources:** https://www.wealthsimple.com/en-ca/learn/wealthsimple-savings-account
- **Short angle:** Strengthens briefing idea "Is 2.5% beating anything?" (Canada only). Real-return math: 2.5% interest minus 3.1% inflation is about -0.6% a year, before tax. Only script it once Statistics Canada's own CPI page confirms the inflation number on production day. The CIPF-vs-CDIC point is a good re-hook but must be stated carefully (different protection scheme, not "no protection").
