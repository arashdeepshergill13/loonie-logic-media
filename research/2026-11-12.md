## Amex Platinum (Canada): Priority Pass and Plaza Premium capped from Jan 1, 2027

- **Country:** Canada
- **Category:** Credit cards (benefit cut)
- **What changed:** From January 1, 2027, Canadian Platinum and Business Platinum basic cardmembers get six complimentary Priority Pass visits and six complimentary Plaza Premium visits per calendar year; supplementary Platinum cards get two; supplementary Gold cards get none. Visits can be used for accompanying guests, so a guest uses up a visit; fees apply once the allocation runs out. Unlimited access (plus one travel companion as a free guest) returns once the account reaches $20,000 in eligible purchases in a calendar year; it is usually processed within a week but can take up to 12 weeks, and lasts until December 31 of the following year (Amex's example: effective May 1, 2027, valid to Dec 31, 2028). Amex's FAQ says spend in 2026 already counts. Interest, fees and cash equivalents don't count; returns reduce the total; spend on a previous card doesn't carry over after an upgrade or downgrade. Lead (Prince of Travel, not seen on Amex's pages today): Centurion, Escape and airline lounges in the Global Lounge Collection aren't capped.
- **What it means for a viewer:** A couple who uses a lounge on both legs of one round trip burns four of the six Priority Pass visits. Canadian Platinum holders who can reach $20,000 of eligible 2026 spend by Dec 31 keep unlimited access through the end of 2027; everyone else starts 2027 on the six-visit cap.
- **Status:** verified (visit counts, guest rule, $20,000 threshold, timing and exclusions on the Amex Canada lounge terms; Jan 1, 2027 effective date and "starting in 2026" spend on the Platinum benefits page)
- **Sources:** https://www.americanexpress.com/en-ca/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/terms · https://www.americanexpress.com/en-ca/benefits/travel/the-platinum-card/ · background (first reported Oct 2025): https://princeoftravel.com/news/amex-platinum-lounge-access-changes-2027/
- **Short angle (Canada only):** "Six visits isn't six trips": guests count as visits, so show the round-trip math, then the $20,000 threshold and the Dec 31 date that decides whether 2027 is unlimited. Say "in Canada" on screen. Don't advise spending to hit a threshold; present the rule and the math.

## Hilton Honors American Express Card (Canada): the first Hilton co-brand card in Canada

- **Country:** Canada
- **Category:** Credit cards (new card)
- **What changed:** Amex and Hilton launched a Canadian Hilton card (reported Oct 29, 2026). Annual fee $150. Welcome offer: 60,000 Hilton Honors points and a Free Night Reward after $3,000 of spend in the first 3 months, plus 10,000 points after $500 of spend in month 13 (up to 70,000 points). Earn: 6X at Hilton hotels and resorts, 4X on eligible dining, gas and groceries in Canada, 2X on everything else. Complimentary Hilton Honors Gold status; $40,000 of spend in a calendar year upgrades to Diamond through the end of the following year. Offer ends January 11, 2027.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Canadians now have a Hilton card with Gold status built in for $150 a year. The month-13 bonus means the full 70,000 needs the card kept past the first renewal.
- **Status:** verified (fee, offer, earn rates, status and end date on Hilton's Canada card page). Lead: launch date Oct 29 and the restriction on existing and former cardholders (InsideFlyer).
- **Sources:** https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/credit-cards/canada-card/ · https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/newsroom/articles/canada/canada-hilton-honors-credit-card.html · lead: https://www.insideflyer.com/posts/hilton-launches-its-first-co-branded-card-in-canada/
- **Short angle (Canada only, moderate):** "The 10,000 points you only get in month 13": the welcome offer's real value against the $150 fee paid twice. Name the country on screen; no recommendation to apply.

## IRS 2027 tax inflation adjustments (US)

- **Country:** US
- **Category:** Rules with dates
- **What changed:** IRS release IR-2026-133 (Nov 5, 2026) sets tax-year 2027 figures: standard deduction $16,600 single and $33,200 married filing jointly; the 37% top rate applies above $661,375 single and $793,650 married filing jointly; annual gift exclusion $20,000 (2026: $19,000). The release doesn't include 401(k) or IRA limits.
- **What it means for a viewer:** US viewers can plan 2027 brackets now. The 2027 retirement-plan limits are still unofficial; keep using the 2026 figures ($24,500 401(k), $7,500 IRA).
- **Status:** verified
- **Sources:** https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-releases-tax-inflation-adjustments-for-tax-year-2027-taxpayers-continue-to-benefit-from-the-working-families-tax-cuts
- **Short angle:** weak alone (US tax, outside the core niche). Pair with the 401(k) and IRA limits once IRS posts them.
