## World of Hyatt 2027: milestones from 10 nights, stackable awards

- **Country:** Worldwide (US-based program)
- **Category:** Loyalty programs (hotel)
- **What changed:** Hyatt's release dated Nov 18, 2026 says that from Jan 1, 2027, Milestone Rewards start at 10 qualifying nights or 20,000 Base Points, then come every 10 nights up to 150 nights a calendar year. New choices on the chart include Tier-Qualifying Night Credits (in 1, 2 or 5), a Free Parking Award (stays up to seven nights), a $30 Inclusive Collection spa credit, a $200 Thompson Hotels credit and a $500 premium-suite credit (paid stays of two nights or more); most can be gifted. Eligible awards can be stacked on one booking (for example a Suite Upgrade, Guest of Honor and Free Parking Award together). All awards are valid 365 days from issue. Pay My Way lets members pay each night with a mix of awards, points, cash or Points + Cash. With linked Delta SkyMiles accounts: Delta milestones from 20 nights or 35,000 Base Points, Sky Club passes at 70, 80 and 90 nights, and a path to Medallion status at 100-150 nights.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Hyatt members who stay 10-20 nights a year get rewards they didn't get before, and award certificates become easier to use together. Which award sits at which night count is in a chart we couldn't read tonight, so don't quote a threshold for a specific award yet.
- **Status:** verified (press release). Hyatt's enhancements page didn't load tonight; the release lists no cuts, but the full terms weren't checked.
- **Sources:** https://newsroom.hyatt.com/worldofhyatt2027 · https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/rewards/world-of-hyatt-enhancements.html (not loaded)
- **Short angle:** "Ten nights now earns you something": the low-stay member's gain, plus stacking. Check the enhancements chart first for which award comes at 10 nights.

## Amex UK Platinum: Priority Pass capped at 20 visits from 2028 unless you spend £30,000 in 2027

- **Country:** UK
- **Category:** Credit cards (benefit cut)
- **What changed:** Amex's UK Platinum card page now says that from Jan 1, 2028, the main cardmember gets 20 complimentary Priority Pass visits per calendar year and each Platinum supplementary cardmember 10. Eligible spend of £30,000 or more on the account during 2027 unlocks unlimited visits for 2028; the count resets every Jan 1. Other Global Lounge Collection lounges stay unlimited. Annual fee £650. (Earlier reported only by blogs; first confirmed on an Amex page today.)
- **What it means for a viewer:** The 2027 spend decides 2028 access, so the clock starts in six weeks. £30,000 a year is £2,500 a month on one card.
- **Status:** verified
- **Source:** https://www.americanexpress.com/uk/charge-cards/platinum-card
- **Short angle:** a UK companion to the Canadian lounge-cap Short (`amex-platinum-canada-lounge-cap`) but a different hook: the 2027 spend clock. Use a different scene format from the Canadian one. Say "in the UK" on screen; no recommendation to apply or cancel.
