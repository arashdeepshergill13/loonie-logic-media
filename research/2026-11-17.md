## Canada CPI, October 2026: 3.1%, unchanged

- **Country:** Canada
- **Category:** Interest rates / inflation
- **What changed:** Statistics Canada (released Nov 16): CPI up 3.1% year over year in October, the same as September; up 0.2% on the month. CPI excluding energy 2.0% (2.1% in September). Gasoline up 30.9% year over year, partly a base-year effect; cellular services down 6.2%; rent growth slowed. Next release (November CPI) Monday, Dec 14.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Prices in Canada are still rising about 3% a year, so the Wealthsimple 2.5% savings rate is still below inflation (real return about -0.6 points before tax). Inflation outside energy is near the 2% target, which is the number the Bank of Canada watches before its Dec 9 decision.
- **Status:** verified
- **Source:** https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/261116/dq261116a-eng.htm
- **Short angle:** weak alone (the savings-vs-inflation Short is made). Use as an updated supporting figure.

## SBI Card (India): 1% fee on big transport payments and heavy fuel spend, from Dec 1, 2026

- **Country:** India
- **Category:** Credit cards (new fees)
- **What changed:** SBI Card's customer notice (dated Oct 27, 2026) says that from Dec 1, 2026:
  - Transportation transactions above ₹30,000 (merchant codes 4111, 4784, 4131) carry a 1% fee on the transaction amount. The notice doesn't say whether the fee is on the full amount or only the part above ₹30,000.
  - Once fuel spend in a billing cycle exceeds ₹50,000 (merchant codes 5172, 5541, 5542, 5983), a 1% fee applies to the total fuel spend for that cycle.
  - The notice page doesn't list which cards; a blog tracker (CardAdvisor) says 38 cards. Lead.
- **What it means for a viewer:** A ₹60,000 fuel month on an SBI card costs ₹600 in fees under the stated rule, more than most cards earn back on fuel. Splitting big transport bookings or fuel across another card avoids it.
- **Status:** verified (thresholds, 1% rate, Dec 1 date, merchant codes); card list is a lead
- **Sources:** https://www.sbicard.com/en/customer-notices.page · lead: https://cardadvisor.in/changes?count=160 · https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/sbi-credit-card-extra-fee-on-fuel-and-transport-spends-above-specified-limits-from-december-1
- **Short angle (India only, moderate):** "The fuel fee that hits your whole month": cross ₹50,000 and 1% applies to all of it, not just the excess (worked math: ₹49,000 = ₹0 fee; ₹51,000 = ₹510). Say "in India" and "SBI Card" on screen. Same lesson as the Rogers Short (compare a fee to your earn rate). Expires Dec 1 as a "before it starts" angle; still usable after as an explainer.

## Rogers Red Mastercard (Canada): 5% on Rogers purchases, 1.5x redemption bonus ends Nov 18

- **Country:** Canada
- **Category:** Credit cards (earn up, redemption cut)
- **What changed:** Rogers' card page says that from Nov 18, 2026, cash back rises to 5% on certain eligible Rogers purchases (within the card's annual limits), and all rewards redeem at a flat 1 point per $0.01. The 1.5x Redemption Bonus on eligible Rogers purchases ends, including for transactions posted before that date. Current earn: Red Mastercard 2% with an eligible Rogers service (first $16,000 a year, then 1%); Red World Elite 3% on USD and 2% otherwise with a Rogers service (first $61,000, then 1.5%).
- **Leads (not on the page fetched):** World Elite loses trip cancellation, interruption and delay insurance (Prince of Travel).
- **What it means for a viewer:** Rogers cardholders with Rogers purchases still marked eligible for the 1.5x bonus lose it after Nov 17; combined with the 1.6% bill-payment fee from Jan 17, 2027, the card's best use moves to the 5% earn.
- **Status:** verified (Nov 18 changes as stated on rogers.com; current earn rates)
- **Sources:** https://www.rogers.com/credit-card · lead: https://princeoftravel.com/news/rogers-red-mastercard-changes-november-2026/
- **Short angle:** none (narrow and Nov 18 is tomorrow). Could be one supporting beat in the Rogers bill-fee Short.
