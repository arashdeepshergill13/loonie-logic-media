## Timely Short ideas

1. **Aeroplan points start expiring again on Nov 30, 2026.** After that date, points expire after 18 months with no activity, and earning or redeeming one point resets the clock. Primary TD, Amex and CIBC Aeroplan cardholders and Elite members are exempt. This works for Aeroplan members in any country; say "Aeroplan" clearly. A Short already exists (`videos/aeroplan-expiry-nov-30.mp4`), so don't make another unless it takes a clearly different angle. File: `research/2026-10-30.md`. **Expires 2026-11-29.**
2. **Amex is splitting points by card, in the US and in India.** In India, from Nov 15, 2026, points are kept separately for each Basic Card and moving points between your own cards ends. In the US, every new card gets its own rewards account, and cancelling the last card on an account forfeits its points. The fix in both countries: know which card your points sit on before you close or redeem anything. Name each country on screen. Files: `research/2026-10-31.md` (India) and `research/2026-10-30.md` (US). **Expires 2026-11-14** for the India deadline angle; after that the US-only angle lasts until 2026-12-29.
3. **Rates are turning up again.** The Bank of Canada hiked to 2.5% (Oct 28) and the RBI hiked to 5.5% (Oct 7). The Fed held at 3.75-4% with two votes for a hike (Oct 28). The Bank of England decides on Nov 5 and is at 3.75% now. The Short covers what this does to variable-rate debt and savings rates in each country. Re-check after the BoE decision on Nov 5. File: `research/2026-10-30.md`. **Expires 2026-12-01.**
4. **Your intro savings rate has an end date.** Revolut UK's 5% boosted rate, on balances up to £25,000, ends Dec 4, 2026. Standard-plan savers then drop to 2.9%: on £25,000, that's about £1,250 a year falling to about £725. Worked math on a promo-rate cliff, with the UK named on screen; the lesson applies in any country. File: `research/2026-10-31.md`. **Expires 2026-12-03.**
5. **UK cash ISA limit drops to £12,000 on 6 April 2027** for under-65s. The overall ISA allowance stays at £20,000, and interest on cash held in stocks and shares ISAs will face a 22% charge. UK only. File: `research/2026-10-30.md`. **Expires 2027-04-05.**
