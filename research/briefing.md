## Timely Short ideas

Five verified angles, ranked by urgency and reach. None is strong today: the rate-hike Short is made, so these are for when the queue is empty.

1. **Two miles-expiry clocks (worldwide).**
   - Emirates Skywards miles earned from 1 June 2027 expire three years after the flight or partner activity date, rounded to month-end. Activity doesn't extend them.
   - Aeroplan instead uses an 18-month inactivity rule after its pause ends Nov 30.
   - `videos/aeroplan-expiry-nov-30.mp4` already covers the Aeroplan date, so lead with the contrast ("activity resets one clock but not the other").
   
   File: `research/2026-11-06.md`. **Expires 2027-05-31.**
2. **(Canada only) Is a 2.5% savings rate beating inflation?**
   - Wealthsimple's savings account pays a flat 2.5% (no tiers, minimums or fees), protected by CIPF up to $1 million rather than CDIC, matching the Bank of Canada's 2.5% policy rate.
   - Real-return math: if inflation is 3.1% (September, reported; a lead), 2.5% is about -0.6% a year before tax. Confirm the CPI on Statistics Canada's own page on production day, or drop the inflation beat.
   - Don't feature the leveraged or crypto products from the same event. Say "in Canada" on screen.
   
   Files: `research/2026-11-07.md`, `research/2026-11-06.md`. **Expires 2026-12-09** (next Bank of Canada decision).
3. **(Weak, UK only) The BA Premium Plus break-even.**
   - The British Airways Amex Premium Plus (UK) earns 1.25 Avios per £1, with a £300 fee and the Companion Voucher at £15,000 of spend a year.
   - On £15,000 a year that's 18,750 Avios. The old 1.5 rate is a lead only.
   - Say "in the UK" on screen and research Avios values on production day.
   
   File: `research/2026-11-04.md`. **Expires 2027-01-31.**
4. **(Weak, US only) Southwest now gives status for card spending.**
   - From Jan 1, 2027, every Rapid Rewards card earns 1 tier qualifying point per $1, and new Silver, Gold and Platinum tiers arrive.
   - Companion Pass qualifying points are retired after Dec 31, 2026.
   - Say "in the US" on screen.
   
   File: `research/2026-11-01.md`. **Expires 2026-12-31.**
5. **(Weak, US only) Same points, different value: Chase to Hyatt is now 4:3.**
   - Chase Sapphire Preferred points move to World of Hyatt at 4:3 (the 1:1 rate for older accounts ended Sep 30, 2026), so 40,000 Chase points become 30,000 Hyatt points.
   - Fits the idea bank's "check the transfer ratio before you transfer". Re-check the Chase page on production day.
   
   File: `research/2026-10-30.md`. **Expires 2026-12-29** (recheck due).
