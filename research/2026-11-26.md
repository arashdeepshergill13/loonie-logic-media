## Aeroplan and Uber: new 5,000-point certificate, tighter earn rules from Dec 7

- **Country:** Aeroplan members who have linked Uber (Air Canada's US-edition news page; no markets named)
- **Category:** loyalty program
- **What changed:** Air Canada's Aeroplan news (dated Nov 25, 2026) says that from Dec 7, 2026:
  - a paid Uber One annual membership can earn a 5,000-point Aeroplan Flight Reward Certificate (terms apply; terms not read);
  - Uber Eats grocery and retail orders earn only when the order is $40 CAD or more;
  - UberXL is no longer an eligible ride under the Uber Premium earn rules;
  - the 250-point monthly bonus (ride + restaurant order + grocery/retail order in the same calendar month) ends at the end of 2026.
  No per-dollar earn rates are given on the page.
- **What it means:** small grocery orders and UberXL rides stop earning, and the easy monthly 250 points goes away; the certificate is new value only for people who already pay for Uber One annually.
- **Status:** verified
- **Source:** https://www.aircanada.com/us/en/ado/home/aeroplan/news/aeroplan-and-uber-program-updates.html
- **Short angle:** weak on its own (small amounts). Could work as a "points you stop earning on Dec 7" checklist, only after reading the certificate terms. Not ranked.

- **What changed:** Upgraded Points (Nov 18) reports a JAL award sale booked directly with JAL miles by Nov 30, 2026: economy from mainland US gateways (BOS, ORD, DFW, LAX, JFK, SAN, SFO, SEA) from 18,900 miles one-way for travel Nov 19, 2026 to Feb 28, 2027, with about $414 in taxes and fees shown; Honolulu to Narita, Kansai or Nagoya from 14,000 miles economy, travel to Jul 31, 2027.
