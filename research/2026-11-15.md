## IRS 2027 retirement plan and IRA limits (US)

- **Country:** US · **Category:** rules with dates
- **What changed:** IRS news release IR-2026-136 (Nov 12, 2026) sets the 2027 limits.
  - 401(k), 403(b), governmental 457 and TSP employee deferral: $25,500 (2026: $24,500).
  - Catch-up at 50+: $8,500 (2026: $8,000), so $34,000 in total; ages 60-63: $11,750.
  - IRA contribution limit: unchanged at $7,500; IRA catch-up at 50+ unchanged at $1,100.
  - SIMPLE deferral: $17,500 (2026: $17,000).
  - Roth IRA income phase-out: single and head of household $158,000-$173,000 (2026: $153,000-$168,000); married filing jointly $250,000-$260,000 (2026: $242,000-$252,000).
  - Traditional IRA deduction phase-out for a single filer covered by a workplace plan: $83,000-$93,000.
- **What it means:** US savers can put $1,000 more into a workplace plan in 2027, but the IRA limit doesn't move. Payroll percentages set for 2026 won't automatically reach the new cap.
- **Status:** verified
- **Source:** https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-25500-for-2027-ira-limit-remains-7500 (notice: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-26-66.pdf, not fetched)
- **Short angle (US only):** pair with the 2027 standard deduction ($16,600 / $33,200, file `2026-11-12.md`): "three 2027 numbers that moved, one that didn't." Real math: the extra $1,000 a year at an assumed return, labelled as an assumption.

## Rogers: 1.6% fee for paying the bill by credit card, from Jan 17, 2027 (Canada)

- **Country:** Canada · **Category:** credit cards / fees
- **What changed:** from January 17, 2027, Rogers adds a 1.6% processing charge to every credit card bill payment, Auto-Pay or one-time. Exempt: Quebec customers, select specialized plans (such as Connected for Success) and former Shaw Mobile customers on migrated plans. Direct debit, online banking, cheque, gift or prepaid cards, debit Mastercard and Visa Debit avoid it.
- **What it means:** a card that earns less than 1.6% back on the bill now loses money on it; switching Auto-Pay to direct debit avoids the fee.
- **Status:** verified
- **Source:** https://www.rogers.com/support/billing-accounts/understanding-the-credit-card-processing-charge
- **Short angle (Canada only):** "your rewards card now costs you money on one bill": a $100 monthly bill costs $1.60 a month, $19.20 a year, against what a 1% or 2% card earns on the same bill. Wider money point: compare a surcharge to the card's earn rate before paying any bill by card. Name the country on screen.
