## September inflation confirmed on official pages (Canada, US, UK)

- **Country:** Canada, US, UK
- **Category:** Interest rates (real returns on savings)
- **What changed:** Earlier runs had these figures from secondary pages only. Today each was confirmed on the official release:
  - **Canada:** CPI up 3.1% year over year in September 2026 (3.0% in August). Gasoline was up 27.8%; excluding gasoline, CPI rose 2.2%. Released Oct 19. October CPI comes out Monday, Nov 16.
  - **US:** CPI-U up 3.7% over the 12 months to September 2026 (not seasonally adjusted). Released Oct 14. October CPI is due Nov 10 at 8:30 a.m. ET.
  - **UK:** CPI 3.5% in September 2026 (3.1% in August); CPIH 3.6%. Released 21 Oct. Next release 18 Nov.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Set against the policy rates already on file, cash is roughly treading water or losing ground before tax: Canada 2.5% policy rate (and Wealthsimple's 2.5% savings rate) vs 3.1% inflation; US 3.75-4% fed funds vs 3.7%; UK 4% Bank Rate vs 3.5%. A savings account paying less than inflation loses buying power even though the balance grows.
- **Status:** verified (all three CPI figures and the next release dates).
- **Sources:** https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/261019/dq261019a-eng.htm · https://www.bls.gov/cpi/ · https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices/bulletins/consumerpriceinflation/september2026
- **Short angle:** A multi-country "real return" worked-math Short: your savings rate minus inflation is what you actually earn. Example in Canada: 2.5% minus 3.1% is about -0.6% a year before tax. Use the viewer's own account rate, not the policy rate, as the example; the policy rates are context only. All three figures get replaced within ten days (US Nov 10, Canada Nov 16, UK Nov 18), so recheck the latest release on production day.

## Qatar Airways Privilege Deals: how the monthly discount works (worldwide)

- **Country:** Worldwide (program-level)
- **Category:** Loyalty programs (Avios)
- **What changed:** Qatar's official Privilege Deals pages confirm the mechanism of its monthly Avios discounts (verified): deals are generally released on the 5th of each month; "save up to 25%" on award flights; Qatar-marketed and operated flights only; offered origin-and-destination, so any Qatar destination to or from a featured city can qualify; Avios needed can vary by tier; taxes, fees and surcharges are paid in cash; no Avios earned on these tickets; Flexi awards excluded; not combinable with other Avios offers.
  - The November batch is a **lead**: InsideFlyer (Nov 5) reports book by Nov 14, travel by Dec 31, 2026, with business class from 26,250 Avios one-way from Bucharest, Colombo, Dar es Salaam, Kathmandu or Tbilisi, 37,500 from Kuala Lumpur and 45,000 from Jakarta or Manila; examples Colombo-Frankfurt business 58,500 (from 78,000) and New York JFK-Colombo 63,750 (from 85,000). The official pages fetched today still showed the October batch or "stay tuned", so none of these November numbers is confirmed.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Anyone holding Avios (including Amex MR, Chase or other transfers into Qatar) can check the deals page after the 5th each month; the discount is on the Avios, not the cash taxes.
- **Status:** verified (the mechanism). Lead: the November dates, cities and prices.
- **Sources:** https://www.qatarairways.com/en-am/offers/privilege-deals.html · https://www.qatarairways.com/en-ae/Privilege-Club/fly-with-avios/special-offers.html · lead: https://www.insideflyer.com/posts/qatar-cuts-avios-award-prices-on-selected-routes/
