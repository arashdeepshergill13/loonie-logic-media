## UK Budget 2026: cash ISA limit stays £20,000; the £12,000 cut is dropped

- **Country:** UK
- **Category:** Rules with dates (ISA, savings tax)
- **What changed:** The Budget on Oct 28, 2026 keeps the annual cash ISA limit at £20,000 for everyone, reversing the planned cut to £12,000 for under-65s from 6 April 2027. The Budget overview says secondary legislation follows in November (that line was read from a summary, not verbatim). HMRC's older policy paper on the cut (published Sep 17, 2026) is still online and not marked withdrawn, which is why it looked current.
- **What it means for a viewer:** UK savers don't need to rush £20,000 into a cash ISA before April 2027: the same £20,000 cash allowance carries on. Anything we said about the "last year of £20,000 in cash" is wrong.
- **Status:** verified (HM Treasury press release, Oct 28, 2026)
- **Sources:** https://www.gov.uk/government/news/budget-2026-breathing-space-for-today-hope-for-the-future · https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/budget-2026-overview-of-tax-legislation-and-rates-ootlar/budget-2026-overview-of-tax-legislation-and-rates-ootlar · superseded: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/reduction-in-the-cash-individual-savings-account-isa-limit/cash-individual-savings-account-isa-limit-reduction
- **Action for the channel:** the published Short `uk-cash-isa-limit-cut` now states a rule that won't happen. The creator should review it (pin a correction comment, or unlist it).
- **Short angle (UK only, strong):** the correction plus the new catch: "The cash ISA cut is cancelled, but tax on savings outside an ISA goes up" (see next item). Say "in the UK" on screen.

## UK savings income tax: 24% / 44% / 49% from 6 April 2027

- **Country:** UK
- **Category:** Rules with dates (tax on interest)
- **What changed:** From tax year 2027-28 (from 6 April 2027), the savings basic rate is 24%, higher rate 44% and additional rate 49%. The Treasury release describes it as a further two-point rise across all bands for savings and property income.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Interest earned outside an ISA, above the tax-free allowances, is taxed more from April 2027, which makes the (still £20,000) cash ISA more valuable, not less. Worked example for a Short: £1,000 of taxable interest costs a basic-rate payer £240 in tax at 24%.
- **Status:** verified (Budget 2026 overview, para 1.1; HM Treasury release)
- **Sources:** https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/budget-2026-overview-of-tax-legislation-and-rates-ootlar/budget-2026-overview-of-tax-legislation-and-rates-ootlar · https://www.gov.uk/government/news/budget-2026-breathing-space-for-today-hope-for-the-future
- **Short angle:** pair with the ISA correction above. Re-check the Personal Savings Allowance amounts on gov.uk before quoting any "tax-free up to" figure (not checked tonight).
- **Also in the Budget (summary only, not verbatim, lead):** Lifetime ISA withdrawal charge falls from 25% to 20% for purchases above the property cap from Oct 29, 2026; a First Time Buyer ISA planned before April 2029.
