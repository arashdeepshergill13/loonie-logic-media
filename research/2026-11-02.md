## Aeroplan co-brand cards: 15% off Air Canada flight rewards, new credits, higher fees (Canada and US)

- **Country:** Canada, US
- **Category:** Credit cards / loyalty programs
- **What changed:** Announced by Air Canada on Sep 29, 2026 (Canada) and by Chase on Sep 10, 2026 (US). Earlier runs missed it, so it is recorded here in full.
  - **Canada, from Jan 12, 2027:** every primary Aeroplan co-brand cardholder (Amex, TD, CIBC) gets FLY15OFF, 15% off the points for eligible Air Canada flight rewards. It applies to the **base fare only**, not taxes, fees or surcharges; you must be signed in and book on aircanada.com, the app or the call centre. Until then it runs as a promotion (Sep 10, 2026 to Jan 11, 2027, for travel through Dec 31, 2027). Additional cardholders don't get it.
  - **Canada, AC Wallet credit:** Premium cards $50 on Jan 15 and Jul 15 (up to $100 a year); Core cards $25 twice a year (up to $50); entry cards none. Valid six months, paid Air Canada flights only, not flight rewards. First one Jan 15, 2027.
  - **Canada, other:** 4th Night Free (hotels) and 4th Day Free (car rentals) on all-points bookings become permanent card benefits; Premium cards get Maple Leaf Lounge access worldwide (cardholder plus one guest) and Priority Security for up to eight companions.
  - **Canada, TD fees from Jan 25, 2027** (charged at the first annual fee date on or after that): Aeroplan Visa Infinite $139 to $159 (additional card $75 to $80); Visa Infinite Privilege $599 to $699 (additional $199 to $209); Visa Platinum unchanged. TD says mailed notice comes 30-60 days ahead and existing fee rebates continue.
  - **Canada, Amex Aeroplan Business Reserve from Jan 12, 2027:** $599 to $799; supplementary card $199 to $249; the no-fee supplementary card (renamed Expense Card) goes to $25.
  - **US (Chase Aeroplan card), since Sep 10, 2026:** annual fee $195 USD; 15% off eligible Air Canada flight redemptions; automatic Aeroplan 25K status; up to $100 USD a year in Air Canada statement credits ($50 twice a year); 4th Day Free car rentals from January 2027; limited-time offer of up to 115,000 points (75,000 after $4,000 in 3 months plus 40,000 after $20,000 in 12 months).
- **What it means for a viewer:** If you hold an Aeroplan co-brand card in Canada or the US, Air Canada award seats now cost 15% fewer points on the base fare, but taxes and surcharges are unchanged, so the real saving on a long-haul award is less than 15% of the total cost. Canadian TD and Amex Business Reserve cardholders pay more from January; the new credits offset part of it only if you fly Air Canada on cash fares.
- **Status:** verified (Air Canada terms, TD Visa Infinite and Privilege FAQs, Amex Business Reserve page, Chase release). Lead only: Amex personal Aeroplan Card $120 to $150 and Aeroplan Reserve $599 to $799 (reported by creditcardGenius and iPhone in Canada; no official Amex page found today); CIBC fees not published.
