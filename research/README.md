# Loonie Logic research

Written every evening by Claude's daily research run (Mode 5 of the youtube-channel-content skill).

- `briefing.md`: the rolling summary the Shorts autopilot reads before picking a topic. It holds the timely Short ideas, the current verified facts, and what ended this week.
- `YYYY-MM-DD.md`: what changed that day in loyalty programs, credit cards, interest rates and bank or fintech accounts, with official sources.

Items are marked `verified` (confirmed on an official page) or `lead` (secondary sources only). Public information only.
