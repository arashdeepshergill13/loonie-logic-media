## Amex Marriott Bonvoy cards (Canada): $150 fee and a $3,000 spend hurdle for the free night, from Feb 9, 2027

- **Country:** Canada
- **Category:** Credit cards (fee rise, benefit cut) / Loyalty programs (Marriott Bonvoy)
- **What changed:** Amex Canada's Marriott Bonvoy American Express Card page says that as of Feb 9, 2027 the annual fee is $150 when charged on or after that date (now $120), and a $3,000 annual spend requirement applies to unlock the annual Free Night Award. The page says other benefits also change and points to a product-changes page (not fetched).
- **Leads (Prince of Travel, Nov 10; not on the Amex page fetched today):** status moves from Silver to Gold Elite on the personal and business cards; the free night stays worth up to 35,000 points; current cardholders' 2027 free night needs no spend; accounts opened by Dec 31, 2026 get their first free night with no spend; a new no-fee Marriott Bonvoy Bright American Express Card launches Feb 9, 2027 (3x Marriott, 2x dining, 1.25x other, Silver Elite, no free night).
- **What it means for a viewer:** In Canada the free night stops being automatic: under $250 a month on the card ($3,000 a year) and you pay $150 for status and points with no night attached.
- **Status:** verified (fee $150 from Feb 9, 2027; $3,000 annual spend for the Free Night Award); the rest is a lead
- **Sources:** https://www.americanexpress.com/en-ca/campaigns/marriott-bonvoy/apply-now/ · lead: https://princeoftravel.com/news/amex-bonvoy-cards-changes/
- **Short angle (Canada only):** "Your free night now costs $3,000": the fee rises $30 and the night now needs $250 a month of spend. Worked math: $150 fee against a night capped at 35,000 points (check the cap on Amex's product-changes page first). Say "in Canada" on screen; no recommendation to apply or cancel.

## BMO eclipse Visa Infinite Privilege (Canada): no FX fee, 10 lounge visits, $200 Travel Credit

- **Country:** Canada
- **Category:** Credit cards (benefits added)
- **What changed:** BMO's card page now says the card has no 2.5% foreign transaction fee on eligible purchases, 10 complimentary airport lounge visits a year (Visa Airport Companion), and a $200 Travel Credit each year on a qualifying travel purchase of $500 or more. 5 points per $1 on travel, gas, groceries, health and wellness, and dining; 1 point per $1 on everything else. Income requirement $150,000 individual or $200,000 household (or $50,000 spend on other cards in 12 months).
- **Leads (Prince of Travel, Nov 2):** effective Nov 2, 2026; fee unchanged at $599 (BMO's page didn't display the fee today); 5x caps ($25,000 travel and dining, $20,000 groceries, $15,000 gas and health); 5x only at Canadian merchants; drugstores removed from 5x; welcome bonus up to 200,000 points on $40,000 in 12 months, applications through Oct 31, 2027.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Another Canadian premium card has dropped the 2.5% FX fee, so the FX fee is no longer a reason to carry a second card abroad with this one.
- **Status:** verified (no FX fee, 10 visits, $200 Travel Credit on $500+, 5x categories, income rule); fee, caps, dates and welcome offer are leads
- **Sources:** https://www.bmo.com/main/personal/credit-cards/bmo-eclipse-visa-infinite-privilege/ · lead: https://princeoftravel.com/news/bmo-eclipse-visa-infinite-privilege-card-refresh/
- **Short angle:** weak alone (the FX-fee Short is made). Usable as a supporting example: "the 2.5% fee premium cards are dropping".

## Wealthsimple Visa Infinite+ (Canada): $20 a month, 2% everywhere

- **Country:** Canada
- **Category:** Credit cards / fintech (Wealthsimple)
- **What changed:** Not new this week, but the official terms are now on file (was a NerdWallet lead). $240 a year, billed as $20 a month (annually in Quebec); waived with $100,000+ in individual assets at Wealthsimple or $4,000+ a month in direct deposits. 2% cash back on everything, no categories, no cap; new clients get 5% for their first 5 weeks. 0% foreign transaction fees. Needs a Wealthsimple chequing account and $80,000 personal or $150,000 household income. Insurance includes travel medical up to $1,000,000 and trip cancellation/interruption up to $3,000 per trip. No lounge access (that's the Visa Infinite Privilege). Purchase rate 20.99%. The 10GB data plan reported by NerdWallet isn't on the page.
- **What it means for a viewer:** Without the waiver, the card needs $12,000 a year of spend just for 2% to cover the $240 fee ($12,000 × 2% = $240); every dollar after that is the real earn.
- **Status:** verified
- **Source:** https://www.wealthsimple.com/en-ca/wealthsimple-visa-infinite-card
- **Short angle (Canada only, moderate):** "The $20-a-month card break-even": $12,000 of spend to cover the fee, or $0 fee with $100,000 of assets or $4,000/month direct deposit. Name the country and the card on screen; no recommendation to apply. The creator holds the Visa Infinite Privilege, not this card, so no first-person claim.
