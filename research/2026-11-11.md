## US October CPI: no 12-month inflation figure this month

- **Country:** US
- **Category:** Interest rates (inflation)
- **What changed:** BLS released October 2026 CPI on Nov 10. All items rose 0.4% in October (seasonally adjusted); core (less food and energy) rose 0.3%. BLS did not publish the headline 12-month change for October 2026 because it did not collect October 2025 survey data during the 2025 lapse in appropriations; the tables show a dash for it. The November CPI comes out Dec 10, 2026 at 8:30 a.m. ET.
- **What it means for a viewer:** The latest official US 12-month figure remains September's 3.7%; anyone quoting an "October inflation rate" is estimating. A 0.4% month is about 4.9% annualised (1.004^12 - 1 = 4.9%), which is a calculation, not a BLS figure.
- **Status:** verified
- **Sources:** https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.htm
- **Short angle:** weak. "The inflation number that doesn't exist" is a curiosity, US only, and the real-return Short is already made. Skip unless paired with the Fed's Dec 8-9 decision.
